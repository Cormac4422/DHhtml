Timeline of Aaron Swartz

08/11/1986

 born in Highland Park

1999

 created the website ‘The Info Network’

2000

won the annual ArsDigita Prize for young people who created useful, educational, and collaborative nonprofit websites

2001

joined the Working Group (WG) when he was 14 and helped create the RSS 1.0

2004

attended Stanford University for college, but dropped out after only a year.

2005

created a software company called Infogami which was a template for websites

created his own website called webpy.org as a framework that uses Infogami for any website creator to use.

2006

moved to San Francisco to work on the Wired Magazine. (found it miserable)

volunteered as an editor for Wikipedia.

2007

helped Simon Carstensen launch Jottit  (a website for creating simple web pages by simply typing into a textbox) using Infogami.

2008

downloaded and released about 20% of the Public Access to Court Electronic Records database from the United States federal court documents.

2010

founded the Demand Progress, an organization aimed to use petitions in order to create a legal movement against internet censorship.

Late 2010-Early 2011

Swartz downloaded a very large number of articles from JSTOR in MIT

06/01/2011

arrested and charged with35 years in prison and a fine of one million dollars, with wire fraud, computer fraud, illegal obtaining of information from a protected computer and damaging a protected computer, (even though both JSTOR and MIT did not pursue Swartz at all).

11/01/2013

was found dead after hanging himself in his Brooklyn apartment.

08/11/2013

The first celebration of ‘Aaron Swartz Day’
