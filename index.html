Introduction Page

DH Role in presentation- Killian Butler

The group assigned roles, defined a timeframe and brainstorming. Initially as a first year student, while nervous at first, the mutual respect and frank sharing of opinions enabled me to settle into my role within the group and express my views with growing confidence. In analysing the artefact developed, I feel that the two 3D designs were well developed and professionally presented. Further time allotted would have allowed us to add further detail and colour to enhance it further. However overall, it was satisfactory given the timeframe for delivery. On the day of the presentation, one of our group members was on a leave of absence due to their respective scholarship abroad, however they were able to send on their work input for the PowerPoint a few days beforehand, which made it easier for us individually to submit the presentation for our assignment earlier to avoid any short-notice and uncertainty about the assignment essentials and to keep on track of the deadline that was accompanied with the presentation. My role in the group work was collection of each stakeholder’s work, compiling the PowerPoint slides and ensuring the subject matter flowed logically. I ensured and reminded fellow team members regarding the submission date. I felt a great sense of responsibility in ensuring all these tasks were completed on time. Being one of the first presenters I felt challenges in that the first presenter sets the scene for the topic being presented, while also allowing other presenters refine their work further. Additional concerns included ensuring all updated work was saved securely to devices to prevent any data being lost. Overall, my organisational skills were developed because of this groupwork . In future groupwork , because of learning achieved from this assignment I will ensure to clearly label and date any drafts of work to ensure the most recent version is readily available.
